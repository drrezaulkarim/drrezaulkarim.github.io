Research to invention

Ideas become useful
when they meet real constraints.

I work at the intersection of scientific contribution and practical implementation, developing proof-of-concepts and evaluating methods on production-scale clusters.

From a research question
to a working system.

Memory budgets, latency, communication costs, and robustness shape the path from a promising model to a useful application. Those constraints are part of the research problem.

My longer-term ambition is to turn research into inventions and products that make capable AI more accessible across applications.
